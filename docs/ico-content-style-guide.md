# ICO Content Style Guide

Status date: 2026-06-16

## Voice

- Institutional, controlled, and evidence-first.
- Use clear operational language.
- Prefer "planned", "draft", "subject to final terms", and "requires review" where information is not final.

## Avoid

- Return, yield, profit, listing, liquidity, regulatory, or security guarantees.
- "Revolutionary", "risk-free", "guaranteed", or urgency-based investment copy.
- Jurisdiction-specific eligibility claims without legal approval.
- Tokenomics values that do not reconcile to approved source data.

## Consent Copy Rules

- Whitelist request and marketing subscription must be separate.
- Marketing consent must be optional and unchecked by default.
- Privacy acceptance must explain request-processing purpose.
- Marketing copy must explain expected email categories and unsubscribe availability.

## Document States

- Available: final approved material exists and link is active.
- Draft: internal/public preview requiring approval.
- Under review: material exists but is not approved for final publication.
- Not yet published: no active link.
